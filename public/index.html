How we work

Four steps, no surprises in the invoice

Discovery

A short paid sprint: we map the problem, the constraints and the riskiest assumption, and give you a written scope with a fixed price range.

Design

Architecture, data model, interfaces and the security boundary — agreed before anyone writes production code.

Build

Two-week increments with a working deployment at the end of each one. You always have something you can click.

Operate

Monitoring, on-call runbooks and a documented handover — whether we keep running it or your team takes it over.
