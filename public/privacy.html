1. Who we are

This website (vibexsoft.com) is operated by VibeX LLC, a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, USA, with a registered address at 2246 E Semoran Blvd #2036, Apopka, FL 32703, United States ("VibeX", "we", "us").

For data-protection purposes VibeX LLC acts as the controller of the personal data described below. You can reach us at hello@vibexsoft.com.

2. Our approach in one paragraph

This is a brochure website. It has no accounts, no contact forms, no payment flow and no user-generated content. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads no third-party scripts, fonts, pixels or embeds, and does not build advertising profiles. The only personal data that reaches us is (a) technical request data in server logs and (b) whatever you choose to put in an email to us.

3. What we process

3.1 Server logs

Our hosting provider records standard HTTP request data: IP address, timestamp, requested path, HTTP status, user-agent string and referrer. We use this only to keep the service available and to detect abuse. Logs are retained for a short operational period (currently up to 30 days) and are not used to identify individual visitors or combined with other data sets.

3.2 Correspondence

If you email us, we process your email address, your name if you give it, and the content of your message, in order to respond and — if we go on to work together — to negotiate and perform a contract. Business correspondence is retained for as long as the business relationship requires and thereafter as required by applicable accounting and limitation rules.

3.3 Local storage

The site stores one item in your browser's localStorage — your chosen interface language (vx-lang). It never leaves your device, is not a cookie, is not used for tracking, and clearing your browser data removes it.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK visitors)

  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interests: operating and securing the website (server logs).
  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — pre-contractual steps and contract performance: answering your enquiry and any subsequent engagement.
  • Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR — legal obligation: where retention is legally required.

We do not rely on consent, because we do not run any non-essential cookies or trackers.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal data, correction, erasure, restriction of or objection to processing, and portability; and you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. 8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS only, with HSTS, a strict Content-Security-Policy that permits no third-party or inline code, and clickjacking, MIME-sniffing and referrer protections. Access to the hosting and mail accounts is restricted and protected by multi-factor authentication. Vulnerability reports are welcome — see /.well-known/security.txt.
